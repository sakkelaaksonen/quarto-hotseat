Restart

How to play

Make a line of four pieces that share at least one trait: a row, a column or a diagonal.

The pieces

Each of the 16 pieces is light or dark, round or square, tall or short and filled or hollow.

A turn

  1. Pick a piece from the right for your opponent.
  2. Your opponent places it on any free cell.
  3. Then they pick a piece for you, and so on.

Keep track of whose turn it is yourselves.

Quarto!

Completed a line? Press Quarto! right after placing the piece, before picking the next one. Miss it and it is gone: neither player can claim that line later.

Draw

If the board fills up without a Quarto, press Draw.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves between the ? button, the buttons, the pieces and the board.
  • The pieces and the board are one stop each: use the arrow keys to move between pieces or squares.
  • Enter or Space picks the focused piece or places it on the focused square. After picking, focus jumps to the first free square; after placing, to the first available piece.
  • Q shouts Quarto! from anywhere.
  • Esc closes this help.
